How it works

Three colours. No guessing.

The app checks train times and where trains are right now. Then it gives you one simple answer.

1

Find gates near you

Allow your location once. RailwayCross shows every railway gate within 20 km, closest first.

2

Check the trains

The app looks at train times and delays. Then it works out when each train will reach each gate.

3

Read the colour

Green means the gate is open. Orange means a train is close. Red means the gate is closed. The big number shows the minutes.

Help

How to use the app.

Ready in one minute

You do not need an account. There is no sign-up.

  1. Open app.railwaycross.com in your browser. There is nothing to install.
  2. Allow your location when the app asks. It is only used to find gates within 20 km.
  3. Tap a gate in the Nearby cards to see it on the big screen. Tap the big screen to see more about that gate.
  4. Tap the star to save a gate. It will open first next time.
Location is off? The app asks you to turn it on. Then it shows the gates near you.

Works on your phone, tablet or computer.

How the planner guesses closed times

The app knows when a train reaches a gate. It does not know how long the gate stays down. So it guesses: the gate closes 1 minute before the train and opens 4 minutes after. If a train is late, the times move.

Your phone does all the work. Nothing is sent anywhere. Guessed times have the "≈" sign.

  1. Today's gate timeline shows when the gate is closed and when it is open.
  2. Plan ahead lets you pick a time and see if the gate will be open when you arrive.
  3. Next long gap shows the next time the gate stays open for a while.
FAQ

Common questions.

Is RailwayCross a safety system?

No. It is a tool to help you plan. It uses train times and live train data, and both can be wrong. Never cross when a gate, light or siren says stop. Always look and listen yourself.

How does it know a train is coming?

The server compares train timetables with live train positions. Then it works out when each train will reach each gate, including any delay.

How sure is the "closed" time?

The train times come from real data. How long the gate stays down is a guess. We count about 5 minutes for each train. That is why the app uses the "≈" sign.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your saved gate and your settings stay in your browser on this device.

What happens to my location?

The app sends your location to the server only to find gates within 20 km. We collect nothing else, because there is nothing to sign in to.

Will I get alerts?

Settings has switches for gate and delay alerts. They save what you want, but alerts are not switched on yet.

Does it work without internet?

No. It needs internet to show live data. If you are offline, the app tells you so. It does not show old, wrong answers.

A gate near me is missing.

The app only shows gates that are already on our map. If yours is missing, it has not been added yet.

Next time you leave, check the gate first.

No account and no sign-up. Light and dark look. Nothing to install. It works in your browser on any phone or computer.
